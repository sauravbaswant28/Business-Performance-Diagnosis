@ Business-Performance-Diagnosis @

1] Project Overview

Business performance analysis using Excel, Python, and Power BI to investigate declining profit margins and identify actionable business insights.
The project uses Excel, Python, and Power BI to analyze financial performance, identify trends, investigate potential drivers of declining profitability, and present actionable business insights.

2] Business Problem

Why is the company's profit margin declining despite revenue growth, and what actions can management take to improve profitability?

3] Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- Power BI

4] Key Findings
- Total Revenue: ₹56.10 Cr (561.04 M)
- Total Profit: ₹9.63 Cr (96.26 M)
- Overall Profit Margin: 17.16%
- Profit margin declined from 25.18% in January 2024 to 8.86% in December 2025.
- Technology Consulting recorded the lowest service-line profit margin at 8.42%.
- Finance Transformation recorded the highest service-line profit margin at 26.94%.

5] Project Objective

To investigate the factors associated with declining profitability and provide data-driven recommendations to support business decision-making.
